Compliance Quick Rules

Accredify enforces these validation rules during initial file ingestion:

Bloom's Taxonomy Verbs

Avoid vague verbs like "Understand", "Learn", or "Know". Engine will recommend "Design", "Synthesize", or "Evaluate".

Target CO Cardinality

NBA & ABET recommend exactly 5 to 7 Course Outcomes per semester-long credit sequence.

Assessment Coverage

Each CO must be evaluated by at least two distinct continuous evaluation tools (e.g. Midterm + Project).
